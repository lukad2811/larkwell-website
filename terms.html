1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following defined terms apply wherever they are used, capitalised:

the Services
The website design, build, hosting, and/or maintenance services Larkwell provides, as selected by the Customer.
the Site
The website Larkwell builds for the Customer under this Agreement.
the Preview
A no-cost, no-obligation working draft of the Site provided before any payment is made.
the Domain
The web address (e.g. yourbusiness.com.au) registered for the Customer's use, where requested.
the Care Plan
Larkwell's optional monthly subscription covering hosting and small ongoing updates to the Site.
Year One Fee
The Domain's actual registration cost plus a 20% first-year setup fee, invoiced separately from the Site build.
ACL
The Australian Consumer Law, as set out in Schedule 2 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth).

2. What Larkwell does

  1. Larkwell designs and builds the Site for the Customer, and may optionally host and maintain it under the Care Plan.
  2. The specific package, price, and any add-ons that form part of the Services are whatever the Customer selected and paid for, or was invoiced for, at the time of ordering.

3. Free preview, then payment

  1. The Customer may request a Preview at no cost and with no obligation to proceed.
  2. The Customer only pays if they decide to go ahead following the Preview.
  3. Build payments are made securely through Stripe, a third-party payment processor. Larkwell never sees or stores the Customer's card details.

4. Domain names

Where the Customer asks Larkwell to arrange the Domain, the following applies to every Customer equally, whether or not they hold the Care Plan:

  1. Not paid at checkout. The Domain is never part of the Customer's Stripe checkout total. It is handled by a separate invoice.
  2. Year One. Once the Customer approves the exact Domain name, Larkwell registers it and pays for the first year, then emails the Customer an invoice for the Year One Fee.
  3. Availability. If the requested name is unavailable, Larkwell will email the Customer to agree an alternative before registering anything.
  4. Eligibility for .au domains. Australian .au domains (including .com.au) legally require an eligible ABN or ACN. Where the Customer chooses a .au Domain, they must provide a valid ABN/ACN, used solely to verify eligibility and register the Domain, and never disclosed publicly or passed to the payment system.
  5. Handover. Once the Year One Fee is paid, Larkwell hands over the Customer's own registrar account login for the Domain.
  6. After Year One. From the second year onward, the Domain is entirely the Customer's responsibility to manage and renew through their own account. Larkwell does not invoice for, manage, or renew the Domain after Year One, regardless of Care Plan status.
  7. Non-payment. If the Year One Fee is not paid, the account login is not handed over and Larkwell will not renew the Domain; it will lapse at the end of the year Larkwell already paid for.
  8. Third-party registrars. Domains are registered through an independent third-party registrar. Larkwell is not responsible for that registrar's own price changes, policies, or outages.

5. Content you provide

  1. The Customer is responsible for ensuring any text, photos, logos, or other material they supply is theirs to use, or that they have permission to use it.
  2. Larkwell is not responsible for copyright or trademark issues arising from content supplied by the Customer.

6. Ownership

  1. Once the Customer's final build payment clears, the design and content created for the Site become the Customer's to keep and use.
  2. The Domain becomes fully the Customer's to control once the Year One Fee is paid and the account login is handed over (clause 4).
  3. Where the Customer holds the Care Plan, Larkwell hosts the Site to enable fast updates. Hosting does not transfer ownership; the Site remains the Customer's at all times. If the Customer leaves or cancels, Larkwell provides a full copy of the Site files and hosting login within a reasonable time.
  4. General-purpose code, templates, or components Larkwell reuses across multiple client projects remain Larkwell's own tools and are not assigned to the Customer, without affecting the Customer's ownership of their finished Site.

7. Account credentials

Once any account login (registrar, hosting, or otherwise) is handed over to the Customer, keeping those credentials secure is the Customer's sole responsibility. Larkwell is not liable for loss or damage arising from credentials being shared, lost, or left unsecured after handover.

8. Timeframes

Builds are completed within 3 days of Larkwell receiving the Customer's business details and content, unless a different timeline was agreed. In the rare event of illness or an unforeseen circumstance on Larkwell's side, the Customer will be told promptly and offered a revised timeframe or a full refund of any amount already paid.

9. The Care Plan

  1. The Care Plan is billed monthly at the price shown on Larkwell's pricing page.
  2. It may be cancelled at any time, with no exit fee, effective at the end of the current billing period.
  3. It covers hosting and small updates (such as prices, hours, or photos) at Larkwell's discretion; larger requests may be quoted separately.
  4. The Care Plan does not include managing or renewing the Domain, which after Year One is always the Customer's own responsibility (clause 4).

10. Refunds

Because each Site is built specifically for the Customer's business, one-off build payments are generally non-refundable once work has started, except where required by the ACL. If the Customer is not satisfied with their Preview, they are free to walk away before paying, at no cost. Care Plan payments already processed for a billing period are not refunded for partial months. Domain invoices, once the Domain has been registered on the Customer's behalf, reflect a real third-party cost already incurred and are not refundable.

11. GST

Larkwell is not currently registered for GST. Prices and invoices issued under this Agreement do not include a GST component. Should this change, tax treatment will be handled and disclosed as required by law at that time.

Where Larkwell is permitted by law to limit its liability, that liability is limited, at Larkwell's option, to re-supplying the Services or paying the cost of having the Services re-supplied.
