How it works

Five simple steps. You barely lift a finger.

No builders to learn, no jargon, no drawn-out meetings. Step one costs you nothing, and the rest only happens if you want it to. Here is exactly what happens, from your first message to your site going live.

STEP 01

You tell me about your business

Your business name, what you do, prices, opening hours, and any photos or logo you have. No photos yet? I use neat placeholders until you do. Send it by chat, email, or the form below, whichever's easiest.

STEP 02

I build your site, in private

I design and build every page from scratch, matched to your business, on a private link only you can see. It's not public yet, just a draft for your eyes.

STEP 03

You check it, I fix anything

You look through the whole thing and tell me what to change, a colour, a word, a photo, anything. I make the changes for free, up to the rounds your package includes, until you're genuinely happy.

STEP 04

You pick a package and pay

Only once you're happy, you choose your package and pay securely through Stripe, I never see or store your card details. Your domain is registered in your name, not mine, from day one, and it's yours to keep permanently, even if you ever leave Larkwell.

STEP 05

I publish it, live for the world

I connect your site to hosting and point your domain at it, so anyone who types it in can find you, day or night, anywhere in the world. You don't touch anything technical, I handle all of it, and it happens within a week of me having what I need from you, guaranteed.
